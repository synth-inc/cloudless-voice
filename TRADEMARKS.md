## 2. Forks must use their own name and icon

You are welcome to fork, modify and redistribute Cloudless Voice under the
GPL. A "fork" is any application derived from the Cloudless Voice source
code, whether modified or not, including applications distributed under a
different name. This is the same definition as in the Smart Cleanup
license (`libs/transcription-cleanup-swift/LICENSE`). Unless we have given
you permission (section 4), if you distribute a fork you must:

- give it a different name that does not include "Cloudless" or anything
  confusingly similar;
- replace the app icon and any Cloudless logos with your own;
- change the bundle identifier (any `inc.synth.*` identifier, such as
  `inc.synth.Onit` or `inc.synth.Onit.dev`) and the update feed so
  your users do not receive Synth's updates, and Synth's users do not
  receive yours;
- not suggest that your fork is made, endorsed or supported by Synth.

## 3. Uses that are always fine

You may, without asking:

- say truthfully that your fork is "based on Cloudless Voice" or "a fork of
  Cloudless Voice", in plain text, in your description or README;
- refer to Cloudless Voice by name in articles, reviews, tutorials,
  comparisons and academic work;
- build Cloudless Voice from source, unmodified, for your own use; the
  build keeps the Cloudless name and icon, which is fine as long as you do
  not distribute it;
- link to https://www.cloudless.so.

## 4. Uses that need permission

Ask us first if you want to:

- distribute any build (modified or not) under the Cloudless or Cloudless
  Voice name or icon;
- use our marks in a product name, domain name, company name, social media
  handle or app store listing;
- use our logos or icon anywhere, including on merchandise.

## 5. Smart Cleanup

Forks may include the closed Cloudless Smart Cleanup library unmodified,
under its own license (`libs/transcription-cleanup-swift/LICENSE`). You may
describe the feature as "Smart Cleanup by Cloudless" so users understand
which account and subscription it requires. That is a descriptive use and
does not permit using our marks for your app.
